<> From 15:00 GMT on the 14th of September till 07:00 GMT on the 17th of September
<> Take part in events “Battle of Britain” in British and German aircraft and get special Trophies!
<>The largest-scale aircraft battle of WWII - battle for supremacy in the skies of the Great Britain in which the Luftwaffe failed to break neither the air defense of the British Isles nor the spirit of the People. The courageous pilots of the Royal Air Force repulsed the Luftwaffe attacks and prevented the German command from achieving superiority.
<>In the United Kingdom, the Battle for Britain is commemorated on the 15th of September. We invite you to re-enact the events in our game this weekend and get rewards for winning.
<>How to get rewards
<>Take part in the "Battle of Britain" event, and get a trophy for every 5 victories with a German or a British aircraft.
