<>A mysterious briefcase containing the keys to the world’s most destructive arsenal has long been the subject of rumors, speculation, and all sorts of theories. What exactly does it contain? Well, in War Thunder, the answer is clear: prizes from past events and useful consumables!
<> When: From April 9th (11:00 GMT) until May 6th (11:00 GMT) , the “Nuclear Football” is available in the in-game Item Shop for 60,000 Silver Lions.
<>The Nuclear Football can only be purchased by players who have bought at least one researchable vehicle at Rank II or higher on their account.
