Compensations for the inconvenience caused by maintenance on April 18th
Attention! Outdated news format. Content may not display correctly.
19 April 2013
<>
Pilots!
<>
It was reported that after today’s release of a minor update for War Thunder, starting from April 18th 07:00 - 10:00 GMT (12pm - 1am PDT), 10,446 accounts of our players might have some gameplay problems and unwanted consequences affecting their Silver Lions accounts. Given this situation, we had to close the gaming servers of War Thunder for inspection and some bug fixing. To avoid possible problems we also had to roll back the progress of these players to their status as they were before the beginning of the risk period (April 18th 07:00 GMT).
<>
To compensate any possible inconvenience, we decided to grant every player from the group at risk with 150 Golden Eagles .
<>
Also, for all Premium Account subscribers (if it was activated before the mentioned period) we are giving one additional day of Premium for free . One extra day of Premium will be added to the remaining number of your Premium days.
<>
We sincerely apologize to you for any inconvenience this has caused.
War Thunder team.
