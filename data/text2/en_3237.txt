As you might already know, Gaijin CEO and War Thunder chief developer Anton Yudintsev will be meeting with players during gamescom 2015 to talk about the future development of the game and, of course, use the opportunity to answer some questions from our Community in person.
Since not everyone will be able to join us in Cologne, Germany, we started a fun competition, Prize Question , inviting all of our Community to post their questions about Gaijin and War Thunder to Anton - and to win some fine prizes at the same time!
We originally planned to pick five interesting and cool questions, but due to the many questions that have been asked - we changed that number to ten! And now it's up to you to select the one that interests you the most and don't forget: Everyone who participates in the vote can win exclusive access to the soon-to-start Crossout Closed Beta Test !
The answers will be provided in a separate publication shortly after the end of gamescom 2015.
