“Smell of Victory”: Warbonds
26 October 2022
Warbonds
<>The Warbond shop for the “Smell of Victory” Battle Pass season will be open from 26th of October until 25th of January. Don't miss your chance to get premium vehicles: from a Chinese fighter to a French light tank. Pay special attention to the German BV 238 rank II bomber a flying boat with an excellent bomb load and plenty of defensive turrets!
<>The Warbond shop is available to players that have vehicles of rank III and up and is part of the Battle Pass . You can purchase items with the special ‘Warbond’ currency that can be obtained by unlocking Battle Pass levels.
<>Premium vehicles in the new season
<> ★ HMS Jervis [wiki]
<> ★ H-81A-2 [wiki]
<> ★ Grant I (USA) [wiki]
<> ★ Pr.1204 [wiki]
<> ★ BV 238 [wiki]
<> ★ AMX-13-M24 [wiki]
"Luscious Lizzie!", No. 31 Squadron, RAF pinup
<>Decals are represented by the Warbonds Shop trophy 2021-2022 as well as a new “Luscious Lizzie!” pinup (decal by Colin 'Fenris' Muir) . Decorations can be found in the Camouflage decoration trophy which contains random plant decorations that will help in camouflaging your vehicle. The player icons in the corresponding trophy this time feature German tanks. Trophies with random camouflages as before become available as new store levels are opened.
<>Among other things, there are many useful items for sale: boosters, universal backups, wagers and orders, test drives of premium vehicles — even talismans on the highest levels of the shop.
<>Check out the full assortment of available Warbond items for the current Battle Pass season and the terms for purchasing them in the game by opening the Battle Pass window or Shop -> Warbond Shop!
