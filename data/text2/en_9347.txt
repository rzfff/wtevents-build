The Snake Box
29 January 2025
Special
<>The New Lunar Year is coming! The wise snake has created a magic box containing ancient artifacts. What commanders fought for in the past, you can obtain with a handful of silver — may luck smile upon you!
<>The “ Snake Box ” can be purchased from the in-game Item Shop for Silver Lions from January 29th (11:00 GMT) until February 28th (11:00 GMT).
<>This box is available to players who have at least one researchable vehicle at rank II or higher on their account.
<>What’s in the box?
<>The Snake Box contains vehicles and camouflages, decals and decorations, player icons and loading screens from past game events such as Chronicles of World War II and Pages of History, World War and May events, as well as War Thunder birthdays. Plus useful consumables including boosters, universal backups, wagers and orders. Some items from past events have been included for the first time as coupons, meaning you’ll be able to sell them on the Market!
<>The Snake Box costs 60,000 Silver Lions. On February 28th at 11:00 GMT, all Snake Boxes will disappear from your inventory, so be sure to open them before then!

<>All items are immediately credited to your account, but there’s a chance of receiving some of them in coupon form, unless otherwise stated.
