<>Even if you missed some Christmas toys, you can still purchase them in the achievement list until January 29th 11:00 GMT
<>Christmas is almost here and Santa is already preparing his reindeers for the sleigh ride, but the trouble is - we are so well hidden that he simply will not find us! The situation must be urgently fixed, we need all need to show ourselves to him! For sure we can set few tanks on fire, but one beautiful Christmas tree with lights will be more beautiful, more noticeable and more appropriate. You just need to collect the toys and light up the light bulbs on the Christmas tree and that is exactly what we will all do.
<>Gathering Christmas toys
<> From 11:00 GMT on December the 22nd until 07:00 GMT on January the 22nd
<>Gather Christmas toys to get rare vehicles and other valuable prizes.

<> For every 2 “Christmas toys for tankers” you will receive one of the following items: 50-200% SL booster, 50-200% RP booster, 5 random back-up vehicles for ground vehicles of rank 2-5, random camouflage for ground vehicles of rank 2-4, random talisman for ground vehicles of rank 2-4.
<> For every 2 “Christmas toys for pilots” you will receive one of the following items: 50-200% SL booster, 50-200% RP booster, 5 random back-up vehicles for aircraft of rank 2-5, random camouflage for aircraft of rank 2-4, random talisman for aircraft of rank 2-4.
<>How to collect the Christmas toys
<>In order to receive a Christmas toy you need to complete any 3 tasks of 5 available. They remain the same for a specific time period but you have only 48 hours to complete a single task.
<>Tankers
<>Destroy 40/20/14 player controlled enemies (AB/RB/SB).
<>Gain a “Supporting fire" achievement 5 times in a squad.
<>Receive at least 2018 points in 8/4/4 battles (AB/RB/SB).
<>Gain 10 victories with a minimum 70% activity.
<>Destroy 11/6/4 (AB/RB/SB) enemy player controlled vehicles in one battle, whilst driving ground vehicles.
<>Pilots
<>Destroy 30/15/10 player controlled enemies (AB/RB/SB).
<>Gain a “Supporting fire" achievement 4 times in a squad.
<>Receive at least 2018 points in 7/4/4 battles (AB/RB/SB).
<>Gain 10 victories with a minimum 60% activity.
<>Destroy 11/3/2 (AB/RB/SB) enemy player controlled vehicles in one battle, whilst flying an aircraft.
<>Terms
<>To get the Christmas toy for tankers or pilots you need to complete any 3 from 5 tasks for the appropriate type of vehicle. To receive the special, ground vehicles vehicles will only register marks of distinction for tankers and aircraft, only marks of distinction for pilots.
<>The tasks can be completed in any random battle for rank 3-6 vehicles, except enduring confrontation.
<>The tasks will be updated at 11:00 GMT every two days.
<>The tasks are divided into pilot and tanker objectives, but you can complete both and get all the prizes.
<>Camouflages and talismans in supply boxes will be given only for vehicles which are already researched by the player.
<>You can follow your progress in your profile -> achievements -> Festive Quest
<>A Christmas toy has been received if its achievement has turned golden.
<>You can purchase missed Christmas toys in the Achievement tab.
