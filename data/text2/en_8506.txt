German Unity Day: Vehicles for GE and a Decal to celebrate!
3 October 2023
Special
<>For many decades following the end of World War II, Germany was divided into two countries. It was only until 1989 when the process of German unification of the German Democratic Republic and the Federal Republic of Germany began, with the official consolidation starting today, on October 3rd, 1990. This date is celebrated yearly as German Unity Day!
<> The Sea Hawk Mk.100 and Pz.Sfl.Ic are available for Golden Eagles!
<> When: From October 3rd (12:00 GMT) until October 6th (12:00 GMT).
<> Where: Germany > Aviation / Army > Premium vehicles.
<> About the tank
The Pz.Sfl.Ic is an exemplary light tank: it’s lightly armored, but at the same time agile and stealthy. With a maximum speed of 56 km/h, the Pz.Sfl.Ic is an excellent choice for scouting, with its range of ammunition allowing it to deal with other tanks without any major issues: just make sure to shoot first!
<> About the aircraft
The Sea Hawk Mk.100 is the only aircraft at its Battle Rating of 8.0 that has air-to-air missiles, the AIM-9B. Alongside this, the Sea Hawk Mk.100 also features 4 x 20 mm Hispano Mk.V cannons, as well as a good selection of bombs and rockets, making it a fantastic all-rounder against aircraft as well in the CAS role in ground battles!
<> Earn the “German Unity Day” decal!
<>For this day, you’ll be able to get this decal by completing the below task.
<> When: From October 3rd (12:00 GMT) until October 6th (12:00 GMT).
<>
Task: Play 3 battles using German vehicles to receive the “German Unity Day” decal!
“German Unity Day” decal
<> Additional details:
You must use a minimum of rank III.
Your activity must not be below 70%.
You can complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions, [Assault] mode and Helicopter PVE battles.
Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → German Unity Day.
You can find the decal in the “Holidays” tab in the Customisation menu.
