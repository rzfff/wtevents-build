Event
<>Within a month, weekend Realistic Battle pilots will be able to participate in the event tab in different “Enduring Confrontation” missions.
<>From 15:00 GMT on the 12th of October until 07:00 GMT on the 15th of October
<>A series of missions [Enduring Confrontation] for RB at the weekends will continue for the aircraft of rank IV without fixed vehicle setups.
<>Attention! In the experimental event all enemy vehicle markers will be switched off. Markers for allies will be displayed only on a distance not larger than 900m.
<>64 players will participate In a battle in aircraft with a Battle Rating between 5.0 and 6.3 for all nations in the mission [Enduring Confrontation] Civil war in the Chinese location “Zhengzhou”. You can join the [Enduring Confrontation] battle even after the mission has already begun.
<>The logic for the front line in realistic mode has been updated, combat and cargo convoys will appear more often and a new “Air Supremacy” mission is available. More information regarding the improvements in [Enduring Confrontation] can be read in the development blog and in the changelog from 15.05.2018 .
