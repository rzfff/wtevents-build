<>About this ship
<> USS Cowell was one of the first destroyers built by the United States after the end of the Washington and London Treaties, and a participant in the Second World War. She resembles her sister ship, USS Fletcher, but has slightly more firepower with three additional turrets with 40 mm Bofors autocannons and one with 20 mm cannons. She is capable of effectively countering both enemy naval and air forces!
