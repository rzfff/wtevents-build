<>F4U-1A
<>This captured American icon is a true treasure for the Japanese aviation tree! A warprize that offers an entirely new and different style of play to what most Japanese pilots will be used too. Being noticeably heavier than its Japanese contemporaries, the Japanese F4U also offers a devastating battery of 6 x 50.cal machine guns and a powerful radial engine to back that up with. Delivery high speeds and excellent overall performance, this will definitely ensure your Japanese lineups have a true ace card up their sleeves!
<>T-14
<>The T14 assault tank is a joint Anglo-American program designed to get up close and personal and lead the charge into battle! Whilst the Americans were rather new to this concept, the British had experience due to their tank doctrine and this would be reflected by their input into the project. Those familiar with the M4, will be at home with the T14. Think of it as an M4 thats not afraid of taking a few shots, whilst rapidly responding with a few of its own thanks to its fast firing 75mm cannon. Lead the charge into battle with the T14!
