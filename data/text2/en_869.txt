25 September 2014

Pilots and Tankers!
We are very pleased to announce that this year, in celebration of the two-year anniversary of War Thunder , we have decided to do something very special for our players. We will throw a big party to celebrate the birthday of the game in the Cyber Stadium club in Moscow, Russia, on November 1st, 2014, and would be glad to see you being a part of it! There you will be able to meet like-minded people, have an opportunity to chat with the development team and, of course, have a good time on the party itself. The creators of the game will talk about the project’s future development plans and will announce some amazing and important news, that true fans should not miss!
<>
On stage we will also have the final matches of the Gladiators 4x4 ‘Battle of the Eagles’ tournament, where the best teams from all over Russia will gather face to face to fight for victory in Realistic and Simulator aircraft battles! You will also be able to spectate as the strongest tanker teams from Russia and Germany clash together in an epic tank showmatch!
<>
Follow what happens on stage in real time , either as a guest on-site or through the official War Thunder channel on Twitch.TV , which will be streaming the whole event live.
<>
And not only the best pilots and tankers in War Thunder will have a chance to win valuable prizes at our party. All invited guests of the event as well as visitors will have the opportunity to take part in various live contests and lotteries, to win prizes from various partner companies like MSI, Nvidia and ROCCAT.
Due to the fact that the number of places in the club is limited, entrance to the party is granted through tickets. We will make an announcement when the ticket sale starts, so make sure to check our website regularly - as it will be a one of a kind experience!
We will be glad to welcome as many of our loyal fans and players from all over the world as possible and will gladly assist travellers from other countries as best as we can - if you plan to come, just get in touch with us!
<>
See you in Moscow!
