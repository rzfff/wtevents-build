<>Hellcat Mk.II
<>The Hellcat Mk II is certainly not your typical British gentleman, hence why this American F6F-5 provided to Great Britain under the lend-lease program sits in the premium line of his majesty's finest! Armed with a formidable battery of 6 x 12.7mm Browning M2 machine guns, this hero of the pacific can now also grace the skies of the atlantic and mediterranean flying the colours of the Fleet Air Arm. Don't forget your capable arsenal of ground attack ordinance to deal with any mechanised targets on the ground in combined arms battles!
<> AMX-13-M24
<>This tank is the child of two different tank families fused together. It sports an M24 Chaffee turret and the hull from an AMX-13. Since this tank mounts a turret from the M24 Chaffee, it wields a powerful 75mm M6 gun and the AMX-13 hull provides this tank with decent mobility since this is a light tank, so the player shouldn't expect to bounce shots heading your way, though it can provide protection against some higher calibre machine gun or autocannon fire if angled well.
<> We are pleased to make the AMX-13-M24 available with Warbonds for the first time!
