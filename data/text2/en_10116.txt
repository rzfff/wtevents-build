<>Join other players in a 3v3 event (for solo players), or gather a team together and show off your skills and tactics in a 4v4 event (for squads).

<> League System
<>In this tournament, we’re testing an important new feature: a league system. Players who join the event start in the Group Stage but can advance further, all the way to the semi-finals and finals. Here is how it works: results are tallied daily at 09:00 GMT. If a player ranks among the top performers based on points, they begin the next period in a higher league. On the other hand, the lowest-ranked players are demoted at the end of the period, while the rest remain in their current league.
<>Participants will be automatically divided into groups based on their league: the Group Stage features up to 48 participants per group, the quarter-finals have 8, and the final sees two opponents face off, just like in a real tournament! Promotion or relegation depends on your group results; check the leaderboard to see if you’re at risk of dropping out of your current league or if you’re performing well and poised to move up! If there aren’t enough players in your group, you’ll remain in the same league for the next period.
<>This is the first time this kind of system has appeared in the game, so let us know what you think! Since this is a trial run of the league system, you’ll earn the main rewards by collecting points, just as before, while advancing through the leagues will unlock commemorative rewards. Advancing past the Group Stage will earn you the “Football League 2026” title. The best of the best — those who win the final — will be awarded the “Football League 2026 Champion” title. Good luck!
<> Event details
<>You can see the exact conditions for each task and track your progress by clicking on your nickname and going to the Achievements → Tank Football menu.
<>The coupons for the “Dragons Flag” and “Tiger Flag” decal, “M551 Football Camo” coupons and “Golden Football Decoration” coupons can be sold on the Gaijin Market.
<>PlayStation and Xbox players can exchange each coupon for 100 Warbonds.
<> Match rules
<>Each match consists of two halves of 5 minutes each. As with mini-football, only pure playing time is taken into account.
<>If the result is a draw, players receive an additional 2 halves of 1.5 minutes each. If at the end of two halves and extra time it’s still a draw on the scoreboard, victory is not awarded to any team.
<>To win early in a 3 vs 3 match, you must get a 3 goal advantage.
<>To win early in a 4 vs 4 match, you must get a 5 goal advantage.
