27 July 2022
Warbonds
<>The Warbond shop for the “Field Testing” Battle Pass season will be open from 27th of July until 26th of October. Don't miss your chance to get premium vehicles: from a Japanese motor gun boat to a Soviet frontline bomber. Pay special attention to the Italian medium Pz.IV G tank with a great 75mm KwK40 L/48 gun: it's available in the Warbonds shop for the first time.
<>The Warbond shop is available to players that have vehicles of rank III and up and is part of Battle Pass . You can purchase items with the special “Warbond” currency that can be obtained by unlocking Battle Pass levels.

<>Aside from the "Armoured Charmer" Pinup (decal by Colin 'Fenris' Muir), you can find decals in the 2016 Warbond shop stickers trophy. There is also a "Helmets”' trophy that contains a decoration in the form of one of infantry or tank crew helmets from the countries represented in the game, including the all-new Israel helmets. Camouflage with player icons now shows the USSR tankers. The trophies with random camouflages, as before, become available as you open new shop levels.
<>Among other things, there are many useful items for sale: boosters, universal backups, wagers and orders, test drives of premium vehicles — even talismans on the highest levels of the shop.
