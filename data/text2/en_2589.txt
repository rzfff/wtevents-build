28 October 2014
Welcome to the War Thunder Anniversary!
On the 1st of November we celebrate the second anniversary of the public launch of our game. Within these two years we have become the most popular aviation simulation in the world, which is proved by your love, support and two entries in the Guinness Book of World Records: “Most aircraft in a flight simulation game” and “Most popular online flight simulation game” .
<>
In May this year we introduced tank and mixed battles, thus becoming the first large-scale World War II simulator game.
<>
<>
Besides planned specials we would also like to celebrate this event with you in person! This is why we invite you to join us on the 1st of November (12:00 Moscow time) in the Moscow Cyber Stadium .
<>
<>During our celebration, you can:
take part in Live communication with the developers
see the presentation about the future of the project
watch the finals of the Gladiators 4x4.Battle of the Eagles in RB and SB
watch the tank show battle between teams from Russia and Germany
take part in different contests and win War Thunder souvenirs and hardware
join the Game Zone where you can win Golden Eagles
experience the Virtual Reality Zone with Oculus Rift headsets
visit the Art Zone, to print your photo from Instagram and get yourself a caricature
