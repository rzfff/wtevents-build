<>The Air Force of the People’s Liberation Army of China was founded on November 11th 1949. Today, they’re among the worldwide top 3 in terms of personnel and military equipment, and thanks to a modernization program, China’s air fleet will soon consist of exclusively modern aircraft.
<> The Z-19E is available for the Golden Eagles and is here to stay!
<> When: From November 10th (12:00 GMT).
<> Where: China > Helicopters > Premium vehicles.
<> About this helicopter
The Z-19E is a small and maneuverable helicopter that features a ballistic computer, thermal imaging and a large selection of weaponry and flares. A true all-rounder helicopter, the Z-19E is capable of effectively countering both ground and air targets, with its survival aided by a MAW (Missile Approach Warning System) and chaffs and flares.

Task: Play 3 battles using Chinese aircraft to receive the “PLA Air Forces Day” decal!
