we are pleased to present to you an updated assortment in the war bonds shop in April. Complete Battle Tasks and earn War Bonds by simply playing War Thunder and exchange it for many valuable prizes including premium vehicles!
Battle Tasks will be available to you after reaching the 3rd rank of any nation in the game. You can read more about War Bonds and Battle Tasks in the War Thunder wiki.
Good luck and see you on the battlefield!

A captured war prize, the typically British Churchill joins the German Ground Forces arsenal! Mounting a fast firing 57mm QQF 6 Pounder main cannon and sporting durable armour, the Churchill provides quite a contrast to its Panzer III and IV counterparts. Leading the charge, albeit rather slowly, from the front is what the Churchill does best. Co-ordinate with the more mobile Panzerkampfwagens on your team to secure objectives and deliver ordinance with speed and precision to your targets. Boost your German lineup and research progress with this captured treasure!
<> Avenger Mk II
<>
<>
<>
The American Workhorse Torpedo Bomber of the pacific joins His Majesty's Royal Navy Fleet Air Arm! Originally known in British Service as the “Tarpon”, the Avenger Mk II represents the second variant of Avenger in British service. The Avenger Mk II provides the British lineup with a reliable and sturdy carrier-borne aggressor, capable of dispatching both Naval and Ground Targets alike. Equipped with 4 x 500 lb bombs or two choices of Torpedo, flexibility is provided when attacking targets on land or at sea. A stalwart for any Rank II combined battle lineup, be sure to add the Avenger Mk II to your Royal inventory!

Battle tasks: These individual tasks bring variety into the gameplay and prevent situations when all the players in a battle try to complete one specific task ignoring mission objectives that are needed for a victory. They are available for players that have reached the 3rd rank of any nation. For completion, the player will receive Silver Lions and Warbonds - special in-game currency with their own shop, where you can purchase many of different items.
