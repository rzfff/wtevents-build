Marathon "It’s going to be hot!"
Attention! Outdated news format. Content may not display correctly.
1 July 2014
Tankers, we are giving you a chance to participate in a summer tank marathon "It’s going to be hot!" which will give you a unique opportunity to get premium tanks and silver, just by playing War Thunder.
During one week, from 26th of June to the 3rd of July, we are creating exciting tasks, doing these, you can get silver lions and by completing 4 out of 7 tasks, you can earn a premium tank:
The T-26 1-GvTBr or the Pz.Kpfw.II (DAK). (It is possible to earn them both)
For each completed task you can receive 50.000 (up to 700,000).
The T-26-1GvTBr and Pz.Kpfw.II (DAK) tanks are added as premium ground vehicles and they are also available to purchase for 250 . In addition, new players to War Thunder will be able to earn one of the tanks for free in his first battle.
Complete the daily tasks!
Earn free premium tanks and up to 700.000 Silver Lions
