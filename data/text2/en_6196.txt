<>About the Kiyoshimo destroyer
<>Japanese destroyers Yugumo type, built in the early 40s were the upgrade of Kagero type destroyers. Ship of this class can reach maximum speed of 35 knots (64 km/h) and differs from its predecessor by the increased size of the hull, modernized bridge and upgraded armament. The armament of the destroyer consists of three 2x127 mm Type D guns, depth charges, and two quadruple rotating torpedo tubes 610 mm caliber with the Type 93 torpedoes (also known as Long Lances) - the most powerful, long-range and fast-moving torpedoes of its time.
