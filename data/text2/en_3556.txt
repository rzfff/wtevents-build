Shop
Premium vehicles for active War Thunder players!
Pilots and tankers, we now have new offers for you in the renewed “Warbonds” shop. Now, there is the two-turreted “Soviet Giant” - a premium SMK tank and various premium aircraft from different nations.
In order to receive them, just complete daily Battle tasks in War Thunder battles and earn a special currency - the “Warbonds”, which can be used to purchase vehicles previously only obtainable with Golden Eagles!
We would like to remind you that Battle tasks are available for all the players that have reached rank 3 in any nation. Battle tasks provide additional missions that upon completion will give a player an additional reward in Silver Lions and “Warbonds” - the latter of which can be spent in the “Warbonds” shop.
Battle tasks are displayed on the right hand promo block, via which you can also access the “warbonds” shop.
The SMK is a heavy two-turreted tank of rank 2 in the USSR tree. This is a premium vehicle, thus meaning it brings more profit in RP and Silver Lions. The key feature of this tank is the opportunity it gives to fire 2 cannons at once which will give a huge firepower hit.
Pilots and tankers!
