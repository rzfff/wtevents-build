Special
<> Pilots and tankers!
Once again, the “War Thunder: 100” event is available for fans of team deathmatch! This time, however, both pilots and tankers can participate and win some really valuable prizes in the process: two rare vehicles!
From 15.00 GMT on March 4th until 07.00 GMT on March 9th
Win the rare Premium KV-1B tank and XF5F aircraft!
To participate, join the events for either Air or Ground forces:
<>
War Thunder - 100: The Prize Event
Team Deathmatch for Rank III aircraft on the Khalkin-Gol location
<>
War Thunder - 100: The Prize Event
Team Deathmatch for Rank III ground vehicles on the Poland location
The only rule you need to know is that the first team to destroy 100 enemies - wins the battle!
Participating in the event yields valuable prizes, including rare Premium vehicles!
<> The Top-300 players in each leaderboard are guaranteed to receive the KV-1B (Finland) or the XF5F respectively .
<> For every 10 victories in the air forces event, with activity equal to or more than 80%, you will receive an Air Force Supply Box
<> For every 10 victories in the ground forces event, with activity equal to or more than 80%, you will receive an Army Supply Box
<>
