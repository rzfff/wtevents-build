<>From 15:00 GMT on the 28th of September until 07:00 GMT on the 1st of October
<>A series of missions [Enduring Confrontation] for RB at the weekends will begin in the skies above the Ardennes for 32 players - a familiar to pilots mode with prolonged fighting and the possibility of unlimited numbers of respawns. You can join the [Enduring Confrontation] battle even after the mission has already begun.
USA and Britain line-up ▼
<>Spitfire F Mk IX, Spitfire LF Mk IX, Plagis' Spitfire LF Mk IXc, Spitfire F Mk XVI, Spitfire FR Mk XIVe, Tempest Mk V, Typhoon Mk Ib, Typhoon Mk Ib/L, Mosquito FB Mk VI, Lancaster B Mk I, Lancaster B Mk III;
<>P-47D-25, P-47D-28, P-47M-1-RE, P-51D-5, P-51D-10, P-51D-20-NA, B-17G-60-VE, B-25J-1, B-25J-20, P-61A-1, P-61C-1, A-20G-25, Spitfire LF Mk IXc (USA).
Germany line-up ▼
<>Bf 109 G-10, Bf 109 G-14, Bf 109 K-4, Fw 190 A-8, Fw 190 F-8, Fw 190 D-9, Ta 154 A-1, Me 410 A-1, Me 410 A-1/U2, Me 410 A-1/U4, Me 410 B-1, Me 410 B-1/U2, Me 410 B-2/U4, Me 410 B-6/R3, Do 217 M-1, Do 217 N-1, He 111 H-16, Do 217 K-1.
<>The setup can be changed, so please keep a close eye on the list of the available vehicles in the event description.
<>The logic for the front line in realistic mode has been updated, combat and cargo convoys will appear more often and a new “Air Supremacy” mission is available. More information regarding the improvements in [Enduring Confrontation] can be read in the development blog and in the changelog from 15.05.2018 .
