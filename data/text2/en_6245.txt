Event
<> Become the last player alive among dozens of opponents in a shrinking battle area! Pick the loot on the map to defeat all enemies up — blow them up with powerful artillery or repair all modules and restores the crew your tank in the battle. Choose tactics and find your optimal tank that will bring victory in the upcoming tournament with prizes!
<>Battle Royale in War Thunder!
<>From 10:30 GMT on the 28th of June until 07:00 GMT on the 1st of July
<>Take part in the training battles of "Battle Royale" in AB and RB before the start of the tournament " King of the Hill ", which will take place very soon™!
<>Player ratings are based on the place in the Leaderboard, and in the following sessions, opponents with the closest rating are selected for them.
<>Terms:
<>You may participate in vehicles of BRs from 6.0 to 7.0
<>You can only play solo (not as squad)
<>Battle locations: Port Novorossiysk, Abandoned factory, Stalingrad and Finland and for the first time Vietnam.
<>There’s a bonus that can be picked up during battle that includes:
<>Artillery — an extremely powerful strike that destroys anything within a specific radius but it takes a while before the barrage begins. Available for all vehicle types. Is not available at the end of a battle
<>Repair kit - repairs all modules and restores all ammo.
<>Med kit — restores the crew.
<>Fire extinguisher.
<>There are additional rewards for the top 3 players in training battles of "Battle Royale".
