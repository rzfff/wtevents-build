<>Celebrating Valentine’s Day on February 14th is a charming tradition dating back to the Middle Ages. Valentine cards, flowers, and chocolates became symbols of the day in the 19th and 20th centuries. War Thunder has its own Valentines day traditions, there’s a themed decal trophy, a trophy with Valentine-themed decorations and the rare MK-IX “Valentine” tank!

<> About this tank
<>The MK-IX “Valentine” is a British tank supplied to the USSR under Lend-Lease. Its 57 mm gun has good penetration even at long ranges, making it a popular choice for countering German tanks. Its good turret armor and gun depression make it ideal for sniper roles. While its armor can hold up in general, it lacks reverse speed and a machine gun, so very close range engagements can be a little more risky.
