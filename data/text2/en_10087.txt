<>The Regia Marina, the Royal Italian Navy, was founded on March 17th 1861, by the unification of the navies of the Kingdom of Sardinia, Naples, and Tuscany. It fought in the Italo-Turkish War, the First World War, and the Second World War. In 1946, after the proclamation of the Republic, the Navy received its current name, Marina Militare. It celebrates its professional holiday, Festa della Marina, on June 10th, in memory of the sinking of the battleship Szent István during World War I.
