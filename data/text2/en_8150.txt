Sky Guardians trophy with with user-created camouflages
16 March 2023
Market
<>Meet the new Sky Guardians trophy! 38 authors from the Live.WT portal created 40 new camouflages: 17 historical, 21 semi-historical, and only 2 fictional! You can use them on vehicles of any rank, both old and new ones from the Sky Guardians update, while supporting the creators!
<>To earn the new trophy, just keep playing War Thunder. To open it, you’ll need to purchase a key on the Market (income is shared with the authors of the camouflages). The key allows you to get a coupon for a camo, rare vehicle, or Sky Guardians decal. You can either activate the coupon on your account, or trade it on the Market to get Gaijin Coins (GJN). A trophy can be sold as well!
<>Contents in the "Sky Guardians" trophy:
Coupons for rare vehicles
21 camouflage coupons for ground vehicles
16 camouflage coupons for aircraft
1 camouflage coupons for naval vessels
2 camouflage coupon for helicopters
Coupon for the Sky Guardians decal
Sky Guardians Decal
<>
<>To unlock the trophy, you will need a new Sky Guardians key that can be purchased on the Market for GJN. Read more about the Market and Gaijin Coins on the wiki.
