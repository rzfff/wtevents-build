War Thunder has reached its 4th Birthday!​
We are celebrating not only with interesting events, but throughout November, unique premium vehicles will be available in the Warbonds shop will delight even the most experienced players. - join the battle and receive war bonds!
In order to receive them, just complete daily Battle tasks in War Thunder battles and earn a special currency - “Warbonds”, which can be used to purchase vehicles previously only obtainable with Golden Eagles!
<> М8А1
Who ever said beginner ranks couldn’t be interesting? Take in the awesome design of this vehicle, which the boasts characteristics of a tank destroyer that can reach 55 km/h, while equipped with a 75mm cannon. Considering it’s in the first rank of the US tree, the benefits should be clear to all. The M8A1 tank destroyer is for those who wish to practice ‘hurricane dynamics’ in battle; it’s perfect for fast and resolute players and you can count on the the 75mm cannon to dispatch your enemy effectively!
<> D.521 (Britain)
This aircraft is capable of effectively competing against all opponents of the same rank, but remember the most important rule: be careful with your ammunition because you only have 60 shells for your 20mm Hispano 404 cannon. Of course, it is also supported by four 7.5mm МАС 1934 machine guns with 2700 rounds. Use this aircraft’s maneuverability to your advantage. Climb to higher altitudes and use its high acceleration speed to complete dive-attacks effectively. Be sure to proudly shout "Vive la France!" before your attack!
<> А7М1 (NK9H)
Fast, well-armed, and multi-functional; an aircraft loved by all. The A7M1 is the fastest Japanese carrier-based fighter, which is beneficial when facing enemies on the ground which may not expect such a sudden assault. Its considerable armament proves to further this advantage: two 20mm Type 99 mod. 2 cannons (200 shells for each cannon) and two 13.2mm Type 3 machine guns. You may want to keep in mind that you are controlling a Japanese aircraft and so, take extra care in avoiding enemy fire, as at times, they can’t take as much of a beating. This is arguably the one ‘negative’ of this aircraft. In terms of its general performance otherwise, everything is solid.
The pilot is safely protected with bulletproof glass, capable of withstanding hits even from 20mm HE projectiles. This is coupled with 13mm plate of armour. No one should underestimate the effectiveness of a Japanese aircraft, and the A7M1 re-enforces this.
<> ТB-3
While this may be one of the of the most unique and truly epic bombers in-game, that alone will not win a battle. Who is your enemy? At this rank, you can expect mostly biplanes. What are you defences? 5 x 7.62mm turrets. In terms of offence, 3 tonnes of bomb-load capacity allow you to easily eliminate a single bombing point and several smaller targets. Remember: you need to actually ‘reach’ your target. The speed of the “soviet fortress” is very low - only 190 km/h. Additionally, given that it's a huge aircraft, it is quite difficult to miss. And so, stick close to your wingman for extra protection!
<> KV-1B (Germany)
This seemingly invincible tank can be found at rank three of the German tree (despite being a Finnish tank). It goes without saying that the KV-1 is a vehicle which has very good armour. This version is equipped with screens, 25-30mm armour plates protecting most of the vulnerable parts around the vehicle and even so, the mobility remains at a very decent level. The 76mm F-32 cannon is certainly capable of causing some grief to opponents; you may not be able to defeat all enemies, but that’s not necessarily how you win a battle! The KV-1B is a damage farmer and hence, you can expect enemies to become nervous at the very least.
