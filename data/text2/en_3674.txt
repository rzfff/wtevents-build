Il-2 (1942) (Germany) - A legendary attacker in the German research tree. This aircraft may hold a different livery, but it still maintains the same amount of power. Although that may seem underwhelming, it is important to keep in mind that this weaponry was developed from the Browning M1921 machinegun, and in view of its low battle rank, is really something! This is an especially good armament for advanced players. The inherent flexibility of this aircraft is the same as its original Japanese brother, but don’t forget to use the flaps!
