<>Decal coupons for “Country Ball” can already be purchased and sold on the Market! Don’t fail! - the potential winner of the World Cup will clearly be valued above others on the Market!
<>XBox and PS4 players will be able to exchange a few coupons for rarer “Country Balls” tomorrow (30th of June).
<>From 11:00 GMT on the 23rd of June until 11:00 GMT on the 16th of July
<>
<>How is it possible to be a fan without any football attributes? Even in the days of the World Cup, people around you should know which team you are supporting. Collect coupons and exchange them for the decals of the countries participating in the World Cup - let everyone in battle see who the champion is!
<>Tasks for getting “Soccer Balls”:
<>Win in 5 battles with 60% activity or more
<>Destroy 24/12/12 (AB/RB/SB) enemies
<>Participate in 4/2/2 (AB/RB/SB) battles by getting 2,000 scores or more
<>Xbox and PS4 players can trade coupons using the "Soccer ball Exchange Voucher". Every "green coupon" grants 1 voucher, whilst a "blue coupon" grant's 2.
<>For 2 vouchers you can receive one "green coupon" with a "country ball" decal, for 4 vouchers you will receive a "blue coupon" that contains a decal of one of the countries that has reached the play-off stage.
<>
<>After the announcement of the winner in the World Cup 2018 the players who received and activated the decal with the “Country Ball” which became the champion will receive a super prize - 3D decoration "Super Cup"!
<>Terms and conditions
<>Each completed task will bring you one “Soccer Ball”
<>Tasks will be restarted each day at 11 GMT.
<>You can complete the tasks on any vehicle of rank II-VI (inclusive of Naval Battles).
<>The progress of the tasks can be tracked in the Spot the ball.
<>You can exchange 2 “Soccer Balls” for the trophy with coupons in the inventory in the tab Workshop - “Spot the ball”.
<>The rarity of the each decal coupon “Country Ball” directly depends on the position of the team in the World Cup table sheet.
<>Possibility to sell the coupons on the market will be added later.
<>3D decoration "Super Cup" will be issued within 48 hours after the end of the event.
<>For PlayStation4® and Xbox One players the possibility to exchange coupons will be added in the future.
