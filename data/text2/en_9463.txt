The Infantry Backpack!
10 April 2025
Special
<>The sounds of the first tanks and aircraft engines from The Great War gradually fade away now, but even after the end of this large-scale war, the need for resources and support on the battlefield has not gone away! The infantry backpack, an essential attribute to any infantryman, is filled with valuable rewards from past War Thunder events and handy consumables.
<> When: From April 10th (11:00 GMT) until May 7th (11:00 GMT), the “Infantry Backpack” is available in the in-game Item Shop for Silver Lions.
<>The “Infantry Backpack” can only be purchased by players who have bought at least one researchable vehicle at Rank II or higher.
<>What’s in the Infantry Backpack?
<>A large number of prizes from past War Thunder events! From the spring and autumn crafting events, to the April Fools events, football competitions, Friday the 13th event and the new vehicle events that have been running from January to April inclusive.
<>The Infantry Backpack costs 60,000 Silver Lions.
<>“Dragons team driver” profile icon
<>“Interceptor driver” profile icon
<>“Dogfighting Pilot” profile icon
<>“Iron Flame” decoration
<>“Bad Turn” decal
<>“Spikes” decoration
<>In total there are more than a hundred different rewards
<>47 vehicles, including the legendary Object 279 and interesting VT1-2
<>14 camouflages
<>31 decorations
<>65 decals
<>13 player icons
<>8 loading screens
<>2 ship flags
<>All prizes are immediately credited to your account. However, there’s a chance of receiving some of them in the form of a coupon unless otherwise stated.
