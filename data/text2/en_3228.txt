Can't make it to gamescom 2015, but still want to get that burning War Thunder question of yours answered by Gaijin CEO Anton Yudintsev? Then don't panic just yet, as you are not alone!
Based on the overwhelming feedback we've received so far, and the huge amount of people asking for a possibility to get their question answered even if not able to go to Germany in person, we've come up with a fun competition very fitting the occasion: We are looking for the five most popular War Thunder related questions from our Community - and you'll be able to cash in prizes as well!
<> How it works:
<> 1. Ask your War Thunder question in our related topics on the forums or on Social Media.

The more votes a question receives, the more detailed the answer will be - if possible!
