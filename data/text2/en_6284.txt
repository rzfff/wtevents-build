<>The Korean War was the first large-scale military conflict of the “Cold War” period which actively lasted 3 years. We invite you to participate as one of the opposing forces in the conflict and to take an opportunity to compare the vehicles of the strongest opponents.
<>From 15:00 GMT on the 19th of July until 07:00 GMT on the 22nd of July
<>Join the battle for the Korean Peninsula in the sky and on the ground with historical vehicle setups (RB).
<>Available events:
<>[Operation] Korea — аircraft battles
<>US, British and Soviet aviation including jet aircraft will participate in the event.
<>[Domination] 38th Parallel — combined battles
<>US, British and Soviet ground vehicles will participate.
<>There are 3 respawns available. To fly out in aircraft and some ground vehicles you need to earn Respawn Scores. The number of respawns on some ground vehicles is limited for the team.
<>The difficulty level is “World War”. A special marker will appear above the enemy vehicle that has been found by your crew. The usual aircraft marker and “lead” marker for self-propelled anti aircraft guns will be displayed. The external bombing marker (as in AB mode) is available for aircraft.
<> Both events have a 30% SL and RP bonus.
<> The event series in the RB game mode offers participants battles with historical setups. In some of these battles, participants have an opportunity to gain additional bonuses. Events can offer battle formats that are different from random battle modes in War Thunder.
