<>The materials in operation “Shipyard” can now be not only traded on the market but also can be used to help you to win additional prizes or exchanged for warbonds.
<>At 11:00 GMT on the 14th of October the materials will stop being dropped in battles, but you will still have time to dispose of the remaining materials until 11:30 GMT on the 21st of October.
<>In the “Headquarters (trade items)” menu
Win more prizes!
<>
<>Choose the “Shipyard” trophy and use one of the recipes to open it.
<> To increase the chances of getting a prize use recipes that include the production enhancements.
<>If luck wasn’t on your side, the used materials will not disappear and will be converted into warbonds.
Exchange for warbonds
<>
<>Each single material of any type can be exchanged immediately for 2 warbonds.
<>You can also trade all operation “Shipyard” items that are available on the market.
<> Warbonds can be spent on decals, boosters, backup vehicles and also special premium vehicles.
Trade on the market
<>
<>Unnecessary materials and other operation “Shipyard” items can be traded on the market to get Gaijin Coins.
<>Gaijin Coins can be used to purchase items from the War Thunder ingame shop or any other items that are available on the market .
<>Prize vehicles and lottery camouflages will be given out as coupons and can be traded on the market.

<>Terms
<>Any materials not used before 11:30 GMT on the 21st of October will disappear.

<>Prize vehicles and camouflages will be given out as coupons and may be traded on the market.
