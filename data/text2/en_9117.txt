<>German Unity Day: A Tank and Plane for Golden Eagles, plus a Festive Decal!
<>In 1945, the Allies divided Germany into zones, which later turned into two countries, the GDR and FRG. In 1989, the process of their unification began, officially completing on October 3rd 1990. Since then, this date has been Germany’s main national holiday!
<>The Sea Hawk Mk.100 and the Pz.Sfl.Ic are temporarily available for Golden Eagles!
<>The Sea Hawk Mk.100 carrier-based jet fighter and the Pz.Sfl.Ic light tank will be temporarily available for 8,560 and 1,000 Golden Eagles respectively.
<> When: From today until October 7th (12:00 GMT).
<> Where: Germany > Aviation / Army > Premium vehicles.
Sea Hawk Mk.100
<>About the aircraft
<>The Sea Hawk Mk.100 for Germany is a capable of firing air-to-air missiles and use its effective 20 mm cannons in air battles. For ground battles, it’s also a great attack aircraft thanks to its range of bombs and rockets available.
Pz.Sfl.Ic
<>About the tank
<>The Pz.Sfl.Ic is good at scouting and capturing points, and even good at being a tank destroyer in experienced hands. It’s ready to penetrate almost any armor, but it’s also quite fragile! It relies on sneaking around using its decent top speed of 56 km/h.
<>Get the “German Unity Day 2024” decal!
<>For this day, you’ll be able to get this decal by completing the task below.
<> When: From October 3rd (12:00 GMT) until October 7th (12:00 GMT).
<> Task: Play 3 battles using German vehicles at rank III or higher to receive the “German Unity Day 2024” decal.
<>
<>“German Unity Day 2024” decal
<> Additional details:
<>Your activity must not be below 70%.
<>You can complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions, [Assault] mode and Helicopter PVE battles.
<>Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → German Unity Day.
<>You can find the decal in the “Holidays” tab in the Customization menu.
