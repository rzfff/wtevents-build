Bastille Day: A Festive Decal, Discounts and Vehicles for Golden Eagles!
12 July 2024
Shop
Special
<>On July 14th, 1789, the first flame of the Great French Revolution was lit: the Parisians captured Bastille fortress. The day is celebrated as a national holiday with festivals, balls and military parades, including the largest parade on the Champs-Élysées, hosted by the French President.
<>Discounts on French vehicles!
<>To commemorate this date, there’s a 50% discount on a selection of high rank French vehicles.
<> When: From today until July 15th (09:00 GMT).
<>The vehicles receiving the discount are the battleship Courbet , the AMX-30 Super medium tank, the EC-665 Tiger HAP attack helicopter, and the Dassault Milan jet fighter that is also returning temporarily for Golden Eagles!
<>The Milan and S.O.4050 Vautour IIN are temporarily available for Golden Eagles!
<>Two premium French jets, the Milan and S.O.4050 Vautour IIN can be purchased for 4,545 (at a discount) and 8,560 Golden Eagles respectively.
<> When : From today until July 15th (09:00 GMT).
<> Where : France > Aviation > Premium vehicles.
<> About each of these vehicles
<>The Milan is an experimental modification of the Mirage III. This is a multi-roler fighter with a wide range of external weapons and good flight characteristics. Thanks to its good top speed, Milan is able to destroy an enemy base and then outmaneuver opponents in combat.
<>The S.O.4050 Vautour IIN is an excellent choice for attacking enemy bases and ground vehicles. It’s got enough bombs for bombing several bases at once. The Vautour IIN also has access to excellent AS-30 Nord air-to-ground missiles and additional rockets.
<>Earn the “Bastille Day” decal!
<>This decal is available for this day!
<> When: From now until July 15th (09:00 GMT).
<> Task: Play 3 battles using French vehicles at rank III or higher to receive the “Bastille Day” decal.
<> “Bastille Day” decal
<> Additional details:
<>You must use a minimum of rank III.
<>Your activity must not be below 70%.
<>You can complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions, and [Assault] mode.
<>Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → Bastille Day.
<>You can find the decal in the “Holidays” tab in the Customization menu.
