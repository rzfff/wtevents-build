<>Ta 154 A-1
<>A German twin-engine fighter with a powerful frontal armament placed in the fuselage, allowing skilled pilots the opportunity to score quick and easy kills. It is a great alternative to its much lesser cousins, the Ju 88 and Do 217. The pilot enjoys protection from incoming fire thanks to armour protection in the front and rear.
<>This protection and firepower comes at a price though, as it is unable to turn well at low speeds, so keep moving at a good pace and turn only when necessary.
<> The Ta 154 A-1 is available for the first time with Warbonds, and is especially notable as it was created by War Thunder players! Vitaly "NovA29R" Vostokov is the creator of the 3D model, and Michael "__StrafeMike__" Ioannidis created the textures.
<>Mörkö-Morane
<>A Finnish modification of a French fighter, this aircraft can be described as small, nimble, and well armed for its rank. It sports a 12.7mm UB machine gun and two 7.5mm wing mounted machine guns.
<>The Mörkö-Morane is far from the best in its class in terms of performance characteristics, but by no means does it lag behind either. Due to its high top speed, great rate of climb and decent survivability, this aircraft is a blast to fly.
<> We are glad to make the Mörkö-Morane available with Warbonds for the first time!
