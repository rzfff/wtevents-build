<>It’s Valentine’s Day. A time to give your soulmate gifts and spend time together! It’s also a time to decorate your vehicles with a love themed decal and decorations. Just like previous years, we’ve prepared a Valentine’s decal and the opportunity to purchase a trophy with decorations, plus a rare Valentine tank temporarily returns!
<>Earn the “Heart of Roses” decal!
<>Get yourself this love-themed decal by completing the following task.
<> When: From February 14th (11:00 GMT) until February 16th (07:00 GMT).
<>
Task: Play 3 battles using any nation to receive the “Heart of Roses” decal!
<>
<>“Heart of Roses” decal
<>Additional details:
You must use a minimum of rank III.
Your activity must not be below 70%.
You can complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions, [Assault] mode and Helicopter PVE battles.
Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → Valentine’s Day.
You can find the decal in the “Holidays” tab in the Customisation menu.
<>Valentine’s Trophy with Decorations!
<> When: From February 14th (11:00 GMT) until February 16th (07:00).
<>A trophy with themed decorations is available to purchase in the in-game Item Shop for 499 Golden Eagles. This trophy includes the “Love Anchor” ship decoration, plus the “Teddy Bear with a Heart” and “Compound Bow” decorations for ground vehicles. When opening it, you’ll receive a decoration that you don’t yet have.
“Anchor of Love” decoration
“Compound Bow” decoration
“ Teddy Bear with a Heart” decoration
<>The MK-IX “Valentine” is temporarily available for Golden Eagles!
<>For 1,000 Golden Eagles, you can get this rare tank this Valentine’s Day.
<> When: From February 14th (11:00 GMT) until February 16th (07:00).
<> Where: USSR > Army > Premium vehicles.
<>About this tank
<>The MK-IX “Valentine” is a British Valentine IX tank that was delivered to the Soviet Union under Lend-Lease. It’s sluggish and slow, but durable and accurate — a good sniper! A quick reload and a low profile allows it to effectively use cover for long-range shots.
