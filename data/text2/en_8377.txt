<>“Stroyny” and “Neustrashimy” for Golden Eagles
<>From July 28th (12 GMT) until July 31st (12 GMT), the destroyers “Stroyny” and “Neustrashimy” from the Soviet blue water research tree are available for purchase for Golden Eagles.
<> Stroyny
<> Neustrashimy
<>The main calibre of the “Stroyny” is the four 130mm B-13 cannons with three types of shells, effective against different types of enemies. The 76mm auxiliary calibre is also quite powerful for its class and six 37mm anti-aircraft guns will ward off any enemy aircraft and boats.
<>“Neustrashimy” on other hand has only two main calibre turrets but wait! ±180 degrees of traverse, an impressive fire rate and excellent ballistics make these 130mm artillery guns truly versatile. The 45mm anti-aircraft guns can protect not only itself but also any surrounding allies and the running characteristics also allows captains to freely change flanks in the battle.
<>“Great emblem of the Russian Navy” decal
<>From July 28th (12 GMT) until July 31st (12 GMT) play 3 battles using Soviet ships (rank III or higher when activity of not less than 70%) and receive the “Great emblem of the Russian Navy” decal.
<>Complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions and [Assault] mode. Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → Russian Navy Day.
“Great emblem of the Russian Navy” decal
<>You can find the decal in the “USSR → Ships” tab in the Customisation menu.
