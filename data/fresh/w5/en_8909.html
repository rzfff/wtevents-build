The SAV 20.12.48 and a Decal are Available for Swedish National Security Forces Day!
29 May 2024 Special

The Swedish Riksdag approved the founding of the National Security Forces Day on May 29th, 1940 against the backdrop of the outbreak of World War II, although territorial defense units began to be formed earlier. Today, their number exceeds 22,000 personnel, approximately half of the Swedish Army.

The SAV 20.12.48 is temporarily available for Golden Eagles!

This fast-firing TD is available for 1,300 Golden Eagles.

When: From May 29th until May 31st (11:00 GMT).

Where: Sweden > Army > Premium vehicles.

About this tank destroyer

  • The SAV 20.12.48 tank destroyer is very vulnerable to enemy fire, so make sure that you use its mobility to get to positions where you can hunker down without being shot. Its main talking point is its incredible rate of fire and powerful armor-piercing shells. This TD can quickly take effective and unexpected positions and take out several enemies in a matter of seconds thanks to its fast rate of fire!

Earn the “Emblem of the Uppland Regiment” decal!

You can get this decal for this commemoration!

When: From May 29th until May 31st (11:00 GMT).

Task: Play 3 battles using Swedish vehicles at rank III or higher to receive the “Emblem of the Uppland Regiment” decal.

“Emblem of the Uppland Regiment” decal

Additional details:

  • You must use a minimum of rank III.
  • Your activity must not be below 70%.
  • You can complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions, and [Assault] mode.
  • Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → Swedish National Security Forces Day.
  • You can find the decal in the “Swedish tanks” tab in the Customization menu.

Vehicle Review: SAV 20.12.48 and its Decal!

When: From May 29th until June 12th (11:00 GMT).

Task: Play 3 battles using SAV 20.12.48 to receive the “Wiki approved: SAV 20.12.48” decal.

“Wiki approved: SAV 20.12.48” decal

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​Additional details:

  • You can complete the task in random battles, except for Enduring Confrontation missions, and [Assault] mode.
  • Track your progress in the hangar by clicking Nickname → Achievements → Holidays → Swedish National Security Forces Day.
  • You can find the decal in the “Wiki” tab in the Customization menu.
