Pz.Sfl.Ic (Germany, rank II)

About the Pz.Sfl.Ic

  • The Pz.Sfl.Ic is an experimental light tank built during the Second World War based on the chassis of the VK 9.01. It’s fitted with a two-man turret housing a 50mm Pak 38 cannon, requiring a complete stop to fire accurately. However, the tank is extremely mobile and capable of penetrating almost any enemy at its rank with ease!

Bf 109 Z-1 (Germany, rank IV)

About the Bf 109 Z-1

  • The Bf 109 Z-1 is an experimental twin-fuselage fighter armed with four 30 mm MK 108 cannons. Scoring hits with these guns is no easy task, making the Bf 109 Z-1 best suited for hunting bombers and other large, sluggish targets especially at high altitudes, where the fighter can reach high speeds.

Z20 Karl Galster (Germany, rank III)

About the Z20 Karl Galster

  • The Z20 Karl Galster is a Second World War veteran featured in the game in a late-war configuration boasting an excellent anti-aircraft suite comprising thirteen 20 mm autocannons. Its main armament consists of five 128 mm guns, while its secondary armament includes six 37 mm Flak M42 mounts. The destroyer is also equipped with eight 533 mm torpedoes.
