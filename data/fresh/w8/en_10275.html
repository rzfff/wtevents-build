Watch the 2026 Grand Finals and Earn Tricolor Smoke & More Twitch Drops!
22 October 2026 Esports

2026 has been a year of fierce competition and unforgettable moments! The War Thunder Championship Series has continued to evolve and so have its teams. Over months of intense battles across air, ground, and combined arms formats, competitors have put their skill, teamwork, and versatility to the test.

Now, eight qualified teams face their final challenge: the 2026 Grand Finals. With $15,000 in prize money on the line, the stage is set for one last showdown. Who will rise above the competition and claim the prestigious title of 2026 Grand Champion?

Before we introduce the eight finalists, let’s take a look at the season standings following this year’s three Majors!

Leading the standings are DODEC, who have delivered another outstanding season, winning Major I and securing a third-place finish at Major III.

Close behind are Voyager, whose rise throughout the season culminated in an impressive Major III victory. With momentum on their side, they head into the Grand Finals as serious title contenders.

In third place are podium regulars AVReS, whose consistent results secured their qualification in convincing fashion. Hot on their heels are Jinx, a new name for 2026, but a roster packed with experience. Their versatility and strong performances earned them fourth-place finishes at most of this year’s Majors.

FIVESTICKS finish the regular season in fifth place after delivering a thrilling performance at Major II, where they claimed second place. That event’s winners, INVICTUS, take sixth in the overall standings, with their Major II title helping secure their spot in the Grand Finals.

Completing the lineup are MRCLS and LEET. Both teams boast experienced rosters, and while they enter this year’s finals as underdogs, they have every intention of shaking up the standings and fighting for the crown.

We also want to congratulate LEGION, Orion, Astra, Scammers, and Ouroboros for earning points in this year’s Pro League. We look forward to seeing them return even stronger in 2027!

Team DODEC

Strategy, Precision, Ambition

Founded in late 2023, DODEC quickly established themselves as a powerhouse, becoming the only team to win Majors across air, ground, and combined arms formats. The 2025 Grand Finals champions approach every battle like a game of chess: strategy first, then execution.

They started 2026 with a dominant Major I victory, defeating MRCLS 3–0 in the final, before adding a third-place finish at Major III. Led by Forze and banujekツ, this international roster now returns to defend its crown.

Team VOYAGER

From Friends to Champions

Founded in May 2024 by a group of friends playing for fun, VOYAGER quickly turned ambition into results, finishing the 2025 WTCS season as runners-up. Through roster changes, their founding core has remained united.

Despite a challenging start to 2026, they reached the top eight at all three Majors, before claiming Major III victory in the 4v4 Tank format. Led by EinsCute, alongside co-captains Erchenn and Arseniywelcam, they enter the Grand Finals with momentum and their sights set on the crown.

Team AVReS

Built to Win Everywhere

Since arriving on the scene in summer 2024, AVReS have developed into a formidable force across air and ground battles. Winners of WTCS TITAN 2025 and WTCS Armored Apex 2025, they finished third overall in the 2025 season.

In 2026, they reached the playoffs at all three Majors, securing second place at Major III. With RasTafaRaY leading the tankers and isawyourunaway commanding the pilots, AVReS bring a balanced roster and a clear ambition to the Grand Finals: first place in air, ground, and combined arms.

Team JinX

Veteran Experience, Relentless Aggression

Formed after the 2025 Grand Finals by three former IgnisAvis players, JinX may be a new name, but their roster brings years of experience at the highest level of War Thunder esports. Their shared approach? Always play aggressively.

A fourth-place finish at Major I set the tone for a consistent debut season, with playoff appearances at all three Majors. Led by La1R and 1N1ck, who exchange leadership duties for air battles, JinX now have their sights set on a Grand Finals podium finish.

Team FiveSticks

The Comeback Story

Founded in 2018, FiveSticks first made their name in propeller aircraft duels before stepping away from competition around 2020. Revived by OpsBan65_ in February 2025, the team has since become a regular presence in top-tier tournaments.

Their comeback gained momentum in 2026, with playoff appearances at all three Majors and a standout second-place finish at Major II. Combining patient pilots with aggressive tankers, FiveSticks now head into the Grand Finals with a clear target: a place in the top three.

Players to watch: OpsBan65_ and C0COJAMBO, who finished second and third respectively in the Major II MVP rankings. The captain behind the team’s revival is also leading by example in battle.

Team Invictus

Newcomers Who Conquered the Skies

Bringing together experienced competitors and rising talent from six nations, Invictus built their roster around teamwork and versatility. Their debut season has already made a powerful statement.

Despite missing the playoffs at Majors I and III, they soared to victory at Major II, collecting 130 points and briefly taking the lead in the season standings. That outstanding performance secured their place in the Grand Finals, where Extatic and Leozander will lead their pursuit of another title.

Player to watch: Leozander, MVP of Major II, whose decisive Kh-38MT strike from a Su-30SM delivered one of the tournament’s standout moments.

Team MRCLS

The Return of Miracles

After disbanding in 2025, Miracles returned for 2026 with a fresh roster and renewed ambition. Competing under the MRCLS name, they wasted no time making their presence felt.

A determined lower bracket run at Major I saw them defeat LEET 3–2 to reach the grand final, where they fell to DODEC. That runner-up finish secured their Grand Finals qualification. Led by v1nnу and skyline地平, MRCLS now have another opportunity to challenge the champions who denied them the trophy.

Team LEET

The Old Guard

One of the longest-standing teams in the competition, LEET bring experience, strategy, and discipline to the battlefield. Despite a difficult season, these veterans have earned their place among the eight finalists.

Their third-place finish at Major I proved decisive, with their run ending in a close 3–2 defeat to MRCLS in the lower bracket final. Led by the versatile VoLKoDaV, LEET now return with a chance to settle that rivalry and one clear ambition: claim the title.
