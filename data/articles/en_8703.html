Meet Airborne General, the 14th Season of the Battle Pass!
22 January 2024 Battle Pass

The M551 Sheridan light tank was named in honor of General Philip Sheridan, an American Civil War General. Equipped with a 76mm M32 cannon, this tank is the main reward of the 14th Battle Pass Season in War Thunder!

New! You’ll now automatically receive all Battle Pass rewards that you earn, even if you forget to claim them on time. At the end of the season, all unclaimed rewards will be credited to your account when you first log into the game. This new feature has already come into effect, which means that you’re guaranteed to receive your earned rewards in the current 13th Battle Pass Season, which ends on January 24th.

To receive Battle Pass rewards, you’ll need to regularly log into the game and complete tasks and challenges. Each level of the Battle Pass will earn you a prize, be it a player icon, loading screen, title, decals, decorations, or 3 brand-new premium vehicles!

When: From January 24th until April 24th.

Season awards

We’ve got a new set of decals, decorations, a profile icon and vehicles lined up for you this season! See the full list in the Battle Pass tab in the hangar, or check them out on our Wiki. Here’s some key rewards for Season 14:

Le Triomphant is now going to be one of the fastest Destroyers in the game. Thanks to its amazing speed, this ship is able to quickly get to advantageous positions on the seas. Combining this with its 4 x 138.6 mm main caliber cannons, you’re going to be giving any enemy at this rank a hard time!

A modernized variant of the IL-2 attack aircraft, equipped with a new engine and a bomb load up to 1000 kg. Fast and easy to use, this aircraft is going to be fun to play in both ground and air battles.

The “Airborne General” Sheridan, who’s given this season its name! This is the M551 Sheridan with the M41 Walker Bulldog’s gun, a two-plane stabilzer and an increased turret traverse speed — perfect for an aggressive playstyle!

  • Emblem of the 1st Marine Infantry Parachute Regiment
  • Emblem of the 101st Airborne Division Headquarters
  • Emblem of the Paramarines
  • Emblem of 9th Paratroopers Assault Regiment
  • Automatic grenade launcher (USA) (tank)
  • Modern Assault rifle (USA) (tank)
  • Fuel Blivet (ship)
  • US Paratrooper

All rewards and challenges for season 14

About the Battle Pass

If you’ve got two Coupon Upgrades from stages in the previous season, then you can claim the new Battle Pass for free! Exchange them in the purchase menu of the Airborne General season.

The Battle Pass is a seasonal event where players can earn many rewards, ranging from Silver Lions and boosters to exclusive season prizes: decorations, profile icons and premium vehicles. The rules are simple: you earn progress points and use them to unlock new stages, earning valuable prizes in the process.

You can unlock new stages and receive awards at any time during the season.

How to participate

Play

  • You get progress points every day for entering the game.
  • From 1 to 5 points.

Complete Battle Tasks

  • See Battle Task descriptions in the game and get progress points for completing them.
  • From 2 to 5 points.

Complete Season Challenges

  • Season Challenges are very similar to Battle Tasks. One Challenge is available at the season’s start. Every week you get another one or two challenges, three more can be unlocked by opening stages.
  • 30 points.
