War Thunder’s Birthday: Discounts and Gifts!
Attention! Outdated news format. Content may not display correctly.
26 October 2018 Special News

It’s amazing how fast time flies! It seems like only yesterday we were flying through the clouds with only the use of aircraft. How far we’ve come; look at us now: War Thunder is a unique project where all fans of military vehicles will find something to their liking. We’ve crossed the six-year milestone, and there are many more milestones yet to come!

Ingame discounts

12:00 GMT on the 26th of October — 11:00 GMT on the 1st of November

  • 50% discount for all modifications, backup vehicles and talismans for all types of vehicles except fleet.
  • 50% discount for premium account on 90 and 365 days.
  • 50% discount for all ground vehicles and aircraft of any nations (except the vehicles of the update 1.81 and 1.83 and the vehicles from Gaijin.net Store).

USA and USSR

From 26th of October (12:00 GMT) till 28th of October (11:00 GMT)

Germany and Britain 

From 28th of October (11:00 GMT) till 30th of October (11:00 GMT)

Japan, France and Italy

From 30th of October (11:00 GMT) till 1st of November (11:00 GMT)

Gifts for all players: Ya-5M Motor Gun Boat

 

From 11:00 GMT on the 1st of November until 07:00 GMT on the 5th of November

  • Win 5 Naval battles with an activity of 50% or higher to get the Ya-5M soviet Motor gun boat! You need to win a battle whilst controlling any ship for a guaranteed receipt of the Ya-5M.
  • Participate in one battle with an activity of 50% or higher in any game mode to get the unique “War Thunder 6 Years” decal .

Returning premium vehicles

To celebrate the anniversary, we are bringing back one premium vehicle to each nation for purchase - they will be available for purchase with Golden Eagles:

  • Be-6 (USSR)
  • Grant Mk I (USA)
  • Sherman Firefly Ic Scorpion (Britain)
  • AMX-13 SS.11 (France)
  • Type 97 Chi-Ha 12cm (Japan)
  • Panzer IV/70 (A) (Germany)
  • Hs 129-B2 Romanian airforce (Italy)

Please note - for those players who bought a premium Hs 129-B2 located in the German research tree, the aircraft will remain there.

Store discounts

 

From the 1st of November until the 7th of November

We continue to celebrate the 6th anniversary of War Thunder and would like to offer you 50% discounts in the Gaijin store for all vehicle packs (except vehicles from the last three updates: 1.79 “Project-X”, 1.81 “Flight of the Valkyries” and 1.83 " Masters of the Sea").

  • 50% discount for the War Thunder vehicle bundles in the PlayStation Store from October 24th till November 7th.
  • 50% discount for the War Thunder vehicle bundles in the XBox Store from November 1st till November 12th.

Details

 

Е-100 and He 162A-1 for tournament winners

From the 4th of November until the 24th of November

And something delicious to celebrate the anniversary! Coupons for the E-100 and the He 162 A1 are waiting for the winners of each tournament. Coupons can be traded on the Market or you can keep them for yourself!

  • Participate in the tournament!
