Legend of Victory: BM-31-12 “Andryusha”
5 May 2023 Event

Meet the bane of the Wehrmacht — MLRS BM-31-12 “Andryusha”! This vehicle undertook its baptism by fire under Novgorod in Russia and helped push the front way back to Berlin — truly a Legend of Victory! You can receive it and many other prizes, including keys from the “Demolition crate” that contains items from past War Thunder events!

From May 10th (11:00 GMT) until May 22nd (11:00 GMT) earn points in battles, unlock stages, and receive prizes!

BM-31-12, Rank III, USSR, Premium

The BM-31-12 will surely please fans of rocket artillery with its powerful strikes that leave no chances of survival for light and medium vehicles. Its warheads carry almost 30 kg of explosives — six times the amount of the legendary BM-13 “Katyusha”! “Andryusha” joined the fight late — in the final year of the war — but it certainly contributed to the Great Victory!

How to earn prizes

Each level unlocks for 35,000 mission points and earns you a new reward.

Score mission points in ground battles of rank III or above in Arcade, Realistic, or Simulator battles. See the score multiplier in the description of the mission, it depends on the gaming mode and maximum rank of the vehicles for the mission.

You may also score points using certain event vehicles of ranks I-II. Such vehicles have event names in their info cards.

Stages change every two days at 11:00 GMT: 12th, 14th, 16th, 18th, and 20th of May.

Your rewards are based on the number of stages you’ve unlocked:

  • For 1 stage: “T-54-412” decal
  • For 2 stages: one of decals + Crowbar
  • For 3 stages: one of decorations + Crowbar
  • For 4 stages: one of decals + Crowbar
  • For 5 stages: one of decorations + Crowbar
  • For 6 stages: premium BM-31-12 “Andryusha”

You can track your progress by clicking on your Nickname → Achievements → Legend of Victory. If you don’t complete a stage in time, until May 25th you will be able to purchase the stages for Golden Eagles and receive the rewards.

Demolition crate

A unique opportunity to receive items from previous War Thunder events, including rewards from Chronicles of World War II, three seasons of World War, Pages of History, War Thunder Birthdays, and last year’s Legend of Victory — over a hundred different prizes in total!

From May 10th (12:00 GMT) until June 5th (12:00 GMT) you can purchase a Demolition crate and a Crowbar to open the crate for Silver Lions in the in-game shop.

Demolition crate contains unique decals, decorations, player icons, loading screens, vehicles, and camouflages. Some items from past events appear for the first time as coupons, and you can trade them on the Marketplace! There are also Silver Lions, boosters, universal backups, wagers, and orders.

The price of a Demolition crate is 10 000 Silver Lions, the price of a Crowbar is 50 000 Silver Lions. You can also receive a Crowbar for unlocking 2, 3, 4, and 5 levels of the Legend of Victory event. On June 5th, 12:00 GMT all Demolition crates and Crowbars will disappear from your inventory, so be sure to use them before that time!

All prizes are immediately activated on your account, but there is a chance to receive some of them as coupons, unless stated otherwise.
