  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Hard Drive: 23.1 GB (Minimal client)
Recommended
  • OS: Windows 10/11 (64 bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i5 or Ryzen 5 3600 and better
  • Memory: 16 GB and more

  • Hard Drive: 75.9 GB (Full client)

  • OS: Mac OS Big Sur 11.0 or newer
  • Processor: Core i5, minimum 2.2GHz (Intel Xeon is not supported)

November 1st — November 3rd | Vehicle giveaway  

Activate a special bonus code WT5YEARS on this page, restart the game, and gain 5 victories to get respective prizes

Terms▼
  • The special will take place from November 1st 11:00 GMT to November 3rd 11:00 GMT  
  • Gain 5 victories whilst driving any ground vehicle and the M8 is yours! (You should be driving a ground vehicle of rank 3 or higher at the end of battle to gain an achievement)
  • Gain 5 victories whilst flying any aircraft - you will receive the I-29! (You should be flying an aircraft of rank 3 or higher at the end of battle to gain an achievement)
  • The achievements can be completed in random battles (they can not be completed in Enduring Confrontation or Assault)  
  • You should use vehicles of rank 3 or higher to unlock the achievement  
  • Your battle activity should be at least 60%

FAQ:▼
  • Q. Do I need a specific amount of GE on my account to get the bonus?
    • No. All the GE that you purchased during the entire time you played War Thunder will count.
  • Q. I don’t remember how much GE I purchased. 
    • PC and Steam players can check it here .
  • Q. I won some GE during specials, do they count?
    • Only purchased GE counts - either as a separate item or as a part of a bundle or a pack.
