Battle Pass: season “River Hunter”
Attention! Outdated news format. Content may not display correctly.
25 October 2021

Get ready for the new season of War Thunder Battle Pass!

Open new stages and get unique combat vehicles, as well as tons of prizes: decals, 3D decorations, profile icons and a unique loading screen and title!

October 27th— January 26th

Battle Pass season 5

Season awards

Every season there are a new set of awards. See the full list in the Battle Pass tab in the hangar, or check out our wiki. Here are the award vehicles for season 5:

LVT-4/40

Light amphibious tank with a 40mm Bofors gun is very effective both against aircraft and enemy armoured vehicles.

PT-658

A nimble and heavily armed PT boat can be equipped with torpedoes, depth charges and rocket launchers.

Beaufighter Mk I (40-mm) 

Experimental early version of the legendary twin-engine fighter built to test punchy 40-mm cannons.

PT-76-57

The good old PT-76 updated with an automatic 57mm gun and a mechanized ammo rack!

Decals “Commandos Marine” emblem, “Gruppo Operativo Incursori unit emblem”, “Emblem of combat swimmers of the Russian Navy”,  “The emblem of the detachment of combat swimmers of the Bundeswehr”.

Ground vehicle decorations “Underwater Assault Rifle” and “Rebreather”, and naval decoration “Inflatable Boat”

“Combat Swimmer” user profile icon

All the rewards and challenges for season 5

What’s new?

Please note that the Battle Pass is completely free to claim if you obtain two Coupon Upgrades from stages in the previous season. They can be exchanged from the purchase menu for the “River Hunter” season Battle Pass!

Warbond Shop improvements

We have significantly expanded the content of the Warbond Shop and adjusted the assortment so that you can find something interesting at each new level. Read details in the upcoming news about the shop update for the current season.

About the Battle Pass

Battle Pass is a seasonal event during which you will earn progress points and use them to open new levels. Each opened level brings you a valuable reward, from Silver Lions and boosters to exclusive season prizes: decorations, profile icons and premium vehicles.

Open new stages and get awards at any time during the season.

How to participate

Play!

  • You get progress points every day for entering the game.
  • From 1 to 5 points.

Complete Battle Tasks!

  • See Battle Task descriptions in the game and get progress points for completing them.
  • From 2 to 5 points.

Complete Season Challenges!

Season Challenges are very similar to Battle Tasks.

  • One Challenge is available at the season’s start. Every week you get another one or two challenges, three more can be unlocked by opening stages.
  • 30 points.
