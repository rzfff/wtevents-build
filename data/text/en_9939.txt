<>Pearl Harbor is home to the largest base and headquarters of the US Pacific Fleet. The battleship USS Oklahoma was one of Pearl Harbor’s defenders and was destroyed in the Japanese attack on December 7th, 1941, without ever seeing action. We’re ready to change that! Participate in the new naval event, earn the USS Oklahoma, and use her incredible firepower in high-ranked battles!
<> From March 12th (11:00 GMT) until March 30th (11:00 GMT) , earn mission points in Naval Battles, unlock stages and receive rewards!
<> USS Oklahoma
<> An Event Vehicle Battleship for the USA at Rank VI
<>Commissioned back in 1916, the battleship USS Oklahoma largely defined the concept of the US ocean-going battleship for many years to come. The ship served first in the Atlantic, and from 1930 onward, with the Pacific Fleet. On the fateful day of the attack on Pearl Harbor, the ship sustained nine hits from Japanese torpedo bombers and sank in port in just 20 minutes; further repairs proved too expensive. In the game, she’s a true behemoth. Her thick armor and ten 356 mm main guns make her an incredibly dangerous and resilient opponent at any combat range. This comes at the cost of mobility, so quickly changing positions with Oklahoma is near impossible. The pre-war composition of her anti-aircraft battery also incentivizes commanders to stay close to allies who can provide stronger anti-aircraft cover for the giant ship.

<>How to get all rewards
<>Each stage can be achieved for 45,000 mission points, every new stage achieved will give you a new reward.
<> Please note: After finishing a stage, you need to collect the reward manually. To do this, click on your name in-game, go to “Pacific Pearl” and click the “Get Reward” button.
<>You’re able to earn mission points in Naval Battles at Rank III or above in Arcade and Realistic Battles. The number of mission points to complete a stage depends on the game mode and the maximum Rank of the vehicle that you use in battle. There’s a multiplier for earning mission points, which is shown in the task description in-game. Points are only awarded at the end of the battle.
<>You can also earn mission points by using event vehicles that are Rank I and II. Event vehicles that can be used have the name of the event that they were obtainable in on their stat card.
<> Stages change every two days at 11:00 GMT on March 14th, 16th, 18th, 20th, 22nd, 24th, 26th and 28th.
<>Rewards are given when you complete a specific number of stages
<>For 1 Stage: trophy with a random reward
<>For 2 Stages: one of the decal rewards
<>For 3 Stages: trophy with a random reward
<>For 4 Stages: one of the decal rewards
<>For 5 Stages: trophy with a random reward
<>For 6 Stages: USS Oklahoma loading screen
<>For 7 Stages: trophy with a random reward
<>For 8 Stages: non-tradable coupon for USS Oklahoma
<>For 9 Stages: a unique camouflage and 4 random modifications for USS Oklahoma
<> Please note: You can only receive the modifications if USS Oklahoma is activated on your account.
<>Discounts for purchasing stages using Golden Eagles
<>Unfinished stages can be purchased for Golden Eagles, but if you’re very close to completing a stage, its price will be significantly reduced.
<>Let’s say you have earned 85% of the required mission points to complete a stage, then the price of purchasing this stage will be halved. If you’re near the finish line for a stage and are at 95% progress, then the price will be reduced by five times. For example, if you’ve scored 39,000 mission points , then the price to unlock this stage will be 499 Golden Eagles . If you’ve scored 43,000 mission points , then you’ll only need 199 Golden Eagles . Remember to keep this in mind if you cannot complete a stage on time!
<>The Upgrade for a coupon
<>When you complete 8 stages, you’ll receive a non-tradable coupon for USS Oklahoma, using it will immediately activate the vehicle onto your account. If you don’t want the vehicle and would like to sell the coupon on the Market, you’ll need the “ Upgrade for coupon ” item.
<>The “Upgrade for coupon” item can be earned in parallel with the stages throughout the event from March 12th (11:00 GMT) until March 30th (11:00 GMT) for 750,000 mission points.
<>After receiving the “ Upgrade for coupon ” item, you can convert a non-tradable coupon for the vehicle into a tradable one and sell it on the Market. The tradable coupon can be sold after 6 days from the moment it’s created.
<>To make a tradable coupon, select the “ Coupon USS Oklahoma (USA) ” in your inventory and click the “Modify” button.
<>The “ Upgrade for coupon ” item is valid until 11:00 GMT on April 3rd . Until this time, you can use it or exchange it for War Bonds. Afterwards, it’ll disappear from your inventory.
<>Tracking your progress
<>You can track the progress of completing stages and the “ Upgrade for coupon ” item by clicking on your Nickname > Achievements > Pacific Pearl. If you don’t manage to complete a stage on time or would like to purchase a stage, you can do so with Golden Eagles until April 3rd from the Achievements window, which will give you the corresponding rewards.
