<>Free Battle Pass
<>You could get free access to the next, 4th Battle Pass by completing enough stages in the current “Strength Athletics” season!
<>How does it work? Battle Pass rewards include “Upgrade for a vehicle coupon” - this season, for example, they appear at levels 105 and 125. These upgrades can be applied to previously received coupons for season vehicles so that they become tradable on the Market, or you can combine both improvements and get a full Battle Pass for the next season. This can be done from season to season!
<>Changes to Season Challenges
<>Regular Seasonal Challenges (available each week) now do not remain unlockable until the end of the season. You have 3 weeks to complete each challenge.
<>At the beginning of the season, you receive one Challenge. Every odd week, another new one will appear, and every even week - two.
<>At the same time, received Season Challenges (for the 21st, 40th and 60th Battle Pass level), will not be changed - they can be still completed immediately and until the season ends.

<>About the Battle Pass
<>
<>Battle Pass is a seasonal event during which you will earn progress points and use them to open new levels. Each opened level brings you a valuable reward, from Silver Lions and boosters to exclusive season prizes: decorations, profile icons and premium vehicles.
<>Open new stages and get awards at any time during the season.
<>
You can access the “Battle Pass” window through the promo block on the right side of the hangar screen.
<>How to participate
<>Play!
<>You get progress points every day for entering the game.

<>Complete Battle Tasks!
<>See Battle Task descriptions in the game and get progress points for completing them.
<>From 2 to 5 points.
<>Complete Season Challenges!
<>Season Challenges are very similar to Battle Tasks.
