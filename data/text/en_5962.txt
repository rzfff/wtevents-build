<>How to collect the Christmas toys
<>In order to receive a Christmas toy you need to complete any 3 tasks of 5 available. They remain the same for a specific time period but you have only 48 hours to complete a single task (tasks will be updated every 2nd day at 11:00 GMT).
<>You can purchase "Christmas toys” for GE without completing the tasks. You can do it by clicking on your nickname → Achievements → Festive Quest → “Christmas toys for pilots” or “Christmas toys for tankers” menu UNTIL the 17th of January.
<>Pilot tasks to receive “Christmas toys for pilots” (whilst flying of the aircraft of rank 3-6).
<>Destroy 35/18/12 player controlled enemies (AB/RB/SB).
<>Gain 10 victories with a minimum 60% activity.
<>Drop at least 10 tonns of bombs (TNT) on the enemy bases.
<>Destroy 11/4/2 (AB/RB/SB) enemy player controlled vehicles in one battle, whilst flying an aircraft.
<>Gain a “Assists in destroying enemies” achievement 6 times whilst flying an aircraft.
<>Tanker tasks for receiving of the “Christmas toys for tankers” (whilst controlling ground vehicles of rank 3-6).
<>Destroy 40/20/14 player controlled enemies (AB/RB/SB).
<>Gain 10 victories with a minimum 70% activity.
<>Capture 15 points.
<>Destroy 11/6/4 (AB/RB/SB) enemy player controlled vehicles in one battle, whilst driving ground vehicles.
<>Gain a “Assists in destroying enemies” achievement 25 times whilst driving ground vehicles.
<>Terms
<>To get the Christmas toy for tankers or pilots you need to complete any 3 from 5 tasks for the appropriate type of vehicle. To receive the special, ground vehicles vehicles will only register marks of distinction for tankers and aircraft, only marks of distinction for pilots.
<>The tasks can be completed in any random battle for rank 3-6 vehicles, except “Assault” co-op mission.
<>The tasks will be updated at 11:00 GMT every two days.
<>The tasks are divided into pilot and tanker objectives, but you can complete both and get all the prizes.
<>Camouflages and talismans in supply boxes will be given only for vehicles which are already researched by the player.
<>You can follow your progress in your profile -> achievements -> Festive Quest
<>A Christmas toy has been received if its achievement has turned golden.
<>You can purchase missed Christmas toys in the Achievement tab.
<>Reward Coupons can be obtained whether you have received these items previously or not.
<>Reward coupons can be traded on the Market after January 18th. You can check the time in the coupon’s description.
<>PS4 and Xbox players will be able to trade coupons with special 3d decorations and decals until February 14th 07:00 GMT.
