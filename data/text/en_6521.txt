TIS MA
USSR, premium, rank III
<>The escort fighter will change the image of Soviet assault aviation in many ways. This twin-engine monster carries 2х37mm + 2х20mm or 2х45mm + 2х20mm cannons, bombs, two types of missiles and a large calibre defensive machine gun.
QF 3.7 Ram
Britain, premium, rank III
<>The SPG QF 3.7 Ram — is an attempt to combine the excellent 94mm anti-aircraft gun and the reliable chassis of the Ram tank into a self-propelled artillery system. Now Great Britain also has a SPG vehicle with a powerful gun and quick reload.
94 ft PT-811
USA, premium, rank II
<>The 94 ft PT-811 — is an experimental version of the successful American PT-810 torpedo boat. A small but formidable warship that combines all the best in the “mosquito fleet”: high speed, torpedo weaponry and small calibre rapid-fire artillery.
J6K1
Japan, premium, rank IV
<>The premium Japanese naval interceptor fighter with a powerful engine. Excellent flight characteristics, serious weaponry - 6x20 mm cannons and 2x13,2 mm machine guns - all that pilots love about Japanese piston aircraft.
Merkava Mk.2b
USA, rank VI (event vehicle)
<>The Israeli main battle tank has become even better! This is a further development of the Merkava series with enhanced turret protection. An excellent vehicle with high crew survivability, excellent angles of inclination in the armour and a wide range of shells.
Тype 1924 Jaguar
Germany, premium, rank III
<>The Type 1924 Jaguar — German destroyer armed with 105mm rapid-fire guns and torpedoes. It has good speed and maneuverability. The best choice for connoisseurs of balanced and versatile warships.

<>Tasks
<>To get one star ⭐ you need to complete any 3 tasks from an available 5 in vehicles of rank III or higher (for naval forces rank II and higher). Tasks are unchanged and will be updated every 2 days at 11:00 GMT.
<>Pilot star ⭐ — Whilst controlling an aircraft
<>Destroy 40 (20 in RB, 14 in SB) player-controlled enemy vehicles.
<>Get 10 wins (with a minimal activity of 60% or higher).
<>Deal 10 tons of bomb damage to enemy bases.
<>Destroy 11 (4 in RB, 2 in SB) player-controlled enemy vehicles in one battle.
<>Help allies to destroy an enemy vehicle 10 times.
<>Tanker star ⭐ — Whilst driving a ground vehicle
<>Destroy 55 (28 in RB, 19 in SB) player-controlled enemy vehicles.
<>Get 12 wins (with a minimal activity of 70% or higher).
<>Capture 15 points in ground battles whilst driving a tank or flying a helicopter.
<>Destroy 11 (6 in RB, 4 in SB) player-controlled enemy vehicles in one battle.
<>Help allies to destroy an enemy vehicle 30 times.
<>Sailor star ⭐ — Whilst controlling a ship
<>Deal 25,000 damage whilst commanding a naval vessel.
<>Get 10 wins (with a minimal activity of 50% or higher).
<>Capture 10 points in naval battles whilst controlling an aircraft or naval vessel.
<>Destroy 3 enemies with torpedoes whilst controlling an aircraft or naval vessel.
<>Play one battle and finish in first place in naval battles.
<>You are able to purchase any missing stars ⭐ for Golden Eagles even without completing the tasks by clicking on your nickname → Achievements → Operation F.R.O.S.T. → Distinction Mark, until the 16th of January, 2020.
<>Terms
<>To get a “Star ⭐” you need to complete any 3 out of 5 tasks for the specific types of vehicles.
<>Tasks can be completed in random battles in all game modes except “Assault” battles and [Enduring Confrontation] missions in vehicles of rank III-VII (for naval forces rank II-IV). See the specifics for the exact conditions for completing achievements in the game client.
<>Completing tasks will be counted in the destruction and help in the destruction of player-controlled enemy vehicles.
<>Completing tasks “Get 12 wins (with a minimal activity of 70% or higher)” in combined ground battles, the task will be counted with such type of vehicles in which you finish the battle (tank or aircraft of the specified rank). By completing a similar task in naval battles the task will be always counted only for naval vessels.
<>You can follow your progress by clicking on your nickname → Achievements → Operation F.R.O.S.T.
<>The “Star ⭐” will be counted as received if its achievement ★ will be gold colored ⭐.
<>Missed “Stars⭐” are available for purchase in the achievement window until the 16th of January 2020. Don’t make an error when choosing between pilot, tanker and sailor stars.
<>Coupons will be available for trading on the market no earlier than the 17th of January 2020. The time from which the coupon can be traded on the market can be seen in the game description of the coupon. The possibility of trading of each coupon separately depends on the time of its receipt and doesn’t depend on the coupon vehicle.
