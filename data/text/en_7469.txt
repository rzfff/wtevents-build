<> Tasks
<>To get one Star ⭐ you need to perform any 3 tasks from 5 in random battles (in vehicles of rank III or higher).
<>The tasks will not change and will be reset every 2 days at 11:00 GMT.
<> Pilot Star ⭐
<> whilst controlling aircraft :
<>Destroy 40 enemies in AB (20 in RB, 14 in SB).
<> Win 9 battles (with an activity of 70% or higher).
<>Drop 12 tons of Bomb TNT on enemy bases in total.
<>Destroy 12 enemies in one battle (4 in RB or 3 in SB).
<> Help allies 12 times to destroy enemy vehicles.
<> Tanker Star ⭐
<> whilst controlling ground vehicles :
<>Destroy 50 enemies.
<> Win 11 battles (with an activity of 70% or higher).
<> Destroy 10 enemies while on an allied point .
<>Destroy 12 enemies in one battle (6 in RB, 6 in SB).
<>Help allies 25 times to destroy enemy vehicles.
<> Sailor Star ⭐
<> whilst controlling naval vessels :
<>Cause 30,000 damage whilst controlling a naval vessel.
<> Win 7 battles in Naval battles (with an activity of 70% or higher).
<> Destroy 5 enemies while on an allied point.
<>Destroy 7 enemies with torpedoes whilst controlling a vessel or aircraft.
<>Win the battle in first place in naval battles.
<>Highlighted tasks can be performed not only on vehicles of rank III and higher, but also on any event vehicles (even of rank I and II)! Such vehicles has the event name in which it was received in its vehicle card.
<>You can purchase Stars ⭐ with Golden Eagles without even completing a task until January the 13th, 2022 in the menu: Nickname → Achievements → Operation W.I.N.T.E.R. → Tasks for tankers/pilots/sailors
<> Detailed terms
<>The exact terms of the achievement can be found in the game client by clicking on your nickname → Achievements →Operation W.I.N.T.E.R.
<>To receive a Star ⭐ you need to complete any 3 tasks from 5 for the respective type of vehicles.
<>You can complete the tasks in random battles (excluding “Enduring Confrontation” and “Assault”).
<>Tasks highlighted in blue can be completed not only on vehicles of rank III and higher, but also on any event vehicles of lower rank which were obtained as part of major game events. Such as winter and summer marathons, WWII chronicles, BP seasons and craft marathons. In the vehicle cards of such vehicles will be indicated the event names in which it first became available in the game.
<>You can follow your progress and see the detailed tasks description by clicking on your nickname → Achievements → Operation W.I.N.T.E.R.
<>A Star ⭐ will be counted as received if the achievements ★ are coloured gold ⭐.
<>Performing tasks must be completed with the destruction, and assistance in the destruction of player controlled vehicles, NOT AI controlled vehicles.
<>By completing the task “Win 11 times” in combined ground battles, the task will be counted as completed with the type of vehicle in which you finished the battle (ground vehicle or aircraft of the specified rank). By completing the same task in naval battles, the task will always be counted for naval vessels.
<>Missed Stars ⭐ can be purchased in the Achievement window until the 13th of January (11:00 GMT), 2022. Don't make a mistake in choosing between the group of achievements - pilots, tankers and sailors.
<>Prizes received in the form of coupons will be available for trading on the market from 14th of January, 2022. The time from which the coupon can be traded can be viewed in the game in the coupon description. The option of trading each coupon separately depends on the time of its receipt and doesn’t depend on the vehicle coupon.
<>PlayStation and Xbox players will be able to exchange decal coupons for similar ones. The exchange is available in the “Workshop” - Exchange items “Operation W.I.N.T.E.R”.
