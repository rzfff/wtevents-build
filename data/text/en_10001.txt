<>Season 23 of the Battle Pass celebrates Greece! Get decals featuring Air Force unit emblems, the Royal Hellenic Navy flag, decorations featuring an ancient Greek sword and helmet, and a profile icon of Steve Pisanos, a Greek pilot who flew Spitfires in the Second World War with the American Volunteer Squadron. What is the Battle Pass? (click to expand) ▼
<>The Battle Pass is an event that lasts for 90 days where players can earn rewards for completing challenges and earning progress points. Rewards range from Silver Lions and boosters to season-exclusive decals, decorations, vehicles and more. Completing the Battle Pass is simple: you earn progress points that unlock levels, earning valuable things along the way!

Leveling up the Battle Pass & Challenges (click to expand) ▼
<>Leveling up is simple! Gather progress points to unlock more levels. One level is 10 progress points. Points can be obtained through logging into the game, completing Daily Tasks and completing specific Challenges. New Battle Pass Challenges unlock once a week and another three can be obtained through reaching specific levels.

Vehicle History (open spoiler): ▼
<> IJN Suzutsuki
<>IJN Suzutsuki, a destroyer and the third ship in the Akizuki class, was commissioned into the Imperial Japanese Navy in 1943 and immediately entered combat. The ship suffered serious battle damage several times and participated in several major military operations, including the final voyage of the Yamato, where she suffered a direct hit to the bow by a bomb. Repairs were deemed hopeless for the ship.
<>In the game, IJN Suzutsuki is coming as a premium rank III Destroyer. While somewhat less maneuverable than many of her classmates, she is armed with eight 100 mm main guns, carries powerful 610 mm torpedoes, and boasts a large arsenal of anti-aircraft guns and radars to repel air attacks.
<> Spitfire LF Mk IXc (CW)
<> A Premium Fighter for Israel at Rank IV
Vehicle History (open spoiler): ▼
<> Spitfire LF Mk IXc (CW)
<>Meet this premium Spitfire Mk 9 from the Royal Hellenic Air Force, flown by Lieutenant Georgios Smyrniotopoulos. This aircraft boasts the inscription “MYSTRAS”, which is the name of Georgios’ birthplace. The Greek Spitfire LF Mk IXc (CW) is a modification of the legendary British Spitfire with a shortened wing (CW stands for Clipped Wing), which significantly increases the roll rate, while a special engine tuning allows it to reach maximum speeds as early as 15,000 ft! This plane is also excellently armed, with two cannons and four machine guns, plus 250 and 500 lb bombs and a 500 lb AP bomb for use against ships.
<>In the game, the Spitfire LF Mk IXc (CW) comes in as a premium at rank IV in the Israel aircraft research tree. This Spitfire is an easy and enjoyable start to learning Israeli aviation and a wonderful addition to your collection of vehicles for any mode!

What is the Warbond Shop? (open spoiler): ▼
<>The Warbond shop is connected with and runs in tandem with the Battle Pass. Items in the Warbond shop can be purchased with a currency called Warbonds that are earned in the Battle Pass. There are a total of 6 shop levels and each one can be unlocked by reaching specific levels in the Battle Pass. In addition to purchasing with Warbonds, each item from shop level 3 requires a certain number of medals to purchase. Medals can be obtained through completing Special Tasks.
<>The Warbond shop can be accessed by clicking on Shop > Warbond Shop from the hangar or from the Battle Pass window by clicking “Shop”
