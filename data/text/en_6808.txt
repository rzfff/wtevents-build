<>Tasks
<>To get one Star ⭐ you need to perform any 3 tasks from 5 in random battles (in vehicles of rank III or higher and from rank II and higher for fleet). Tasks will not change and will be reset every 2 days at 11:00 GMT.
<>Pilot Star⭐ — for controlling aircraft
<>
<>Destroy 40 enemies in AB (20 in RB, 14 in SB).
<>Win 10 battles (with an activity of 60% or higher).
<>Drop 10 tons of Bomb TNT on enemy bases in total.
<>Destroy 11 enemies in one battle (4 in RB, 2 in SB).
<>Help allies 10 times to destroy enemy vehicles.
<>Tanker Star ⭐ — for controlling ground vehicles
<>Destroy 50 enemies.
<>Win 14 battles (with an activity of 70% or higher).
<>Capture 15 points in ground battles whilst controlling a ground vehicle or helicopter.
<>Destroy 12 enemies in one battle (6 in RB, 6 in SB).
<>Help allies 30 times to destroy enemy vehicles.
<>Sailor Star ⭐ — for controlling fleet
<>
<>Cause 25,000 damage whilst controlling a naval vessel.
<>Win 8 battles (with an activity of 50% or higher).
<>Capture 7 points in naval battles whilst controlling a vessel or aircraft.
<>Destroy 3 enemies with torpedoes whilst controlling a vessel or aircraft.
<>Finish the battle in first place in naval battles.
<>You can purchase the Stars ⭐ for Golden Eagles without even completing the task in the menu: Nickname → Achievements → Operation S.U.M.M.E.R. → Tasks for tankers/pilots/sailors
<>Detailed terms
<>To receive a Star ⭐ you need to complete any 3 tasks from 5 for the respective type of vehicles.
<>You can complete the tasks in random battles (excluding “Enduring Confrontation” and “Assault”) in vehicles of rank III or higher (for fleet - rank II or higher). The exact terms of the achievement can be found in the game client.
<>Performing tasks must be completed with the destruction, and assistance in the destruction of, player controlled vehicles, NOT AI controlled vehicles.
<>By completing the task “Win 14 times” in combined ground battles, the task will be counted as completed with the type of vehicle in which you finished the battle (ground vehicle or aircraft of the specified rank). By completing the same task in naval battles, the task will always be counted for ships.
<>You can follow your progress by clicking on your nickname → Achievements → Operation S.U.M.M.E.R..
<>The Star ⭐ will be counted as received if the achievements ★ are coloured gold ⭐.
<>Missed Stars ⭐ can be purchased in the Achievement window until the 3rd of September. Don't make a mistake in choosing between the group of achievements - pilots, tankers and sailors.
<>Coupons will not be available for trading on the market before the 4th of September. You can see in the game the time from which the coupon can be traded in the coupon description. The possibility of selling each coupon separately depends on the time of its receipt and does not depend on the vehicle on the coupon.
<>From the 10th of August, PlayStation 4® and Xbox One players will be able to exchange decoration and decal coupons for similar ones (you can exchange coupons of the same type). The exchange is available in the “Workshop” - Exchange items “Operation S.U.M.M.E.R. 2020” .
