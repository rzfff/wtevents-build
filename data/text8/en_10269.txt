<>About the HMS Iron Duke
<> HMS Iron Duke served as the flagship of the Grand Fleet during the Battle of Jutland. She inflicted serious damage on the German battleship SMS König and drove off destroyers attempting daylight torpedo attacks. The HMS Iron Duke herself sustained no damage during the engagement. Like many other battleships of the era, she lacks adequate anti-aircraft protection but boasts substantial armor and powerful armament, featuring ten 343 mm main-battery guns and twelve 152 mm secondary-battery guns.
