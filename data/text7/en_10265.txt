Vehicle History (open spoiler): ▼
<> S-706
<>The S-706 torpedo boat was laid down at the Danziger Waggonfabrik shipyard in Danzig and commissioned on October 31st 1944. During the war, it operated in the North Sea and the English Channel. After the war, it was transferred to the United States as a war reparation; it was studied in Philadelphia and subsequently sold for scrap in 1948.
The S-706 belongs to the most advanced generation of German Schnellboote (fast attack craft); it reaches speeds of nearly 80 km/h, carries depth charges, and is equipped with four torpedo tubes, two at the bow and two at the stern, with a total loadout of six torpedoes. It can launch torpedoes both while closing in on a target and while breaking away. Its gun armament includes 30 mm and 20 mm cannons, as well as machine guns. At close range, this arsenal can destroy any enemy boat in mere seconds. All in all, the premium S-706 is set to become one of the best torpedo boats in the game, excelling both in engagements against other small boats and as a point-capturer in battles involving larger naval vessels.

<> Jagdpanther G2
<>Further development of the Jagdpanther tank destroyer led to the 1944 introduction of the second and final production variant: the Jagdpanther G2. These tank destroyers saw extensive use during the final stages of the war, notably in the Lake Balaton Offensive and the Ardennes campaign.
<>The Jagdpanther is a common sight in mid-tier battles, and the reason for its popularity is thanks to an exceptionally successful balance of high firepower, reliable protection, and decent mobility. Its 88 mm gun, the same found on the famous King Tiger, can penetrate the frontal armor of any medium tank and most heavy tanks. The already formidable sloped frontal armor of the fighting compartment easily withstands most smaller-caliber armor-piercing rounds, while angling the hull turns it into a nearly impenetrable barrier. The Jagdpanther G2 variant retains all the strengths of the fearsome G1, adds an Em 0.9 m R rangefinder and offers premium status, making battles with this vehicle even more profitable!

What is the Warbond Shop? (open spoiler): ▼
<>The Warbond shop is connected with and runs in tandem with the Battle Pass. Items in the Warbond shop can be purchased with a currency called Warbonds that are earned in the Battle Pass. There are a total of 6 shop levels and each one can be unlocked by reaching specific levels in the Battle Pass. In addition to purchasing with Warbonds, each item from shop level 3 requires a certain number of medals to purchase. Medals can be obtained through completing Special Tasks.
<>The Warbond shop can be accessed by clicking on Shop > Warbond Shop from the hangar or from the Battle Pass window by clicking “Shop”

<> Z4 Zerstörer (Germany) — first time in the Warbond shop!
<>The destroyer USS Claxton (DD-571) entered service in December 1942 and saw active combat in the Pacific Theater. In December 1959, it was transferred to the West German Navy, where it was renamed Z4 Zerstörer. It stands out from other Fletcher-class destroyers due to its premium status and its secondary armament: six 76 mm guns that have access to high-explosive shells with proximity fuzes. Combined with its radar, this makes the ship a lethal threat to both aircraft and small naval vessels at great distances!
<> FR-1 Fireball (USA) — first time in the Warbond shop!
<>The first jet aircraft to enter service with the U.S. Navy, the Fireball is notable for its hybrid propulsion system, which combines a piston engine with a jet engine. This configuration allows it to achieve higher speeds and outperform piston-engine competitors during maneuvers. It is armed with four 12.7 mm machine guns, and its external hardpoints can carry HVAR rockets, fuel tanks, naval mines, and 500 or 1000 lb bombs.
